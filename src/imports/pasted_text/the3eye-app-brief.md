Design and build THE3EYE as a complete, production-quality, mobile-first road intelligence app.

THE3EYE — “Drive with less noise.”

The app is an India-focused community road-information platform inspired by the best UX patterns from Google Maps, Waze, Mappls and Apple Maps, but DO NOT copy their branding, screens, assets, wording or distinctive designs. Synthesize the best ideas into a unique The3eye experience.

CORE EXPERIENCE:
• Live map showing community-reported traffic checking, cameras, accidents, hazards, road closures, flooding, construction and traffic.
• Reports are GPS + timestamp based.
• Private photo evidence is used for verification/moderation and is never publicly displayed.
• Nearby drivers can confirm “Still there” or “Not there”.
• Reports have High / Medium / Low confidence and naturally expire when stale.
• Strong protection against spam, fake reports and abuse.
• Neutral road-information framing — never encourage evasion, speeding, dangerous driving or obstructing enforcement.

HOME SHOULD BE MAP-FIRST:
• Beautiful custom-feeling map
• Minimal visual noise
• Semantic report colours
• Live status such as “Live · 7 reports nearby”
• Compact driving intelligence panel
• Example: “Traffic checking · 420m ahead · High confidence · 6 confirmed · 48 sec ago”
• Huge thumb-friendly + Report button
• Critical information must never be obscured by UI.

REPORTING MUST BE RIDICULOUSLY SIMPLE:
Open → choose category → confirm location/evidence → submit → done.
Use large touch targets, smart defaults and minimal typing.
Design everything for someone who may be driving.

ALERTS:
“500m — Traffic checking ahead — High confidence · 8 confirmations”
Voice: “Traffic checking reported 500 metres ahead.”
Alerts should be contextual, concise and non-disruptive.

ROUTE MODE:
Show ETA, distance, active alerts on route and simple route intelligence such as “Mostly clear” / “2 active issues”.

PROFILE:
Trust score, reports, confirmations, accuracy, impact, reports/history, vehicles, saved places, preferences, privacy and Premium.

PREMIUM:
“The3eye Pro — Drive smarter. Stay focused.”
Ad-free, advanced voice alerts, custom alert radius, route intelligence, driving statistics, multiple vehicles, advanced map layers and personalization.
Keep the core road information genuinely useful for free users.

VISUAL DESIGN:
Make this SEXY, modern, sophisticated, slightly futuristic and extremely polished — something that looks like a real world-class consumer app, NOT an AI-generated template.

Use excellent typography, spacing, hierarchy, icons, map styling, cards, sheets and micro-interactions.

ANIMATION + MOTION:
Make the app feel alive with fast, subtle, premium native motion:
• smooth bottom sheets
• marker appearance/selection
• map transitions
• confirmation feedback
• report submission animation
• route/ETA transitions
• theme transitions
• subtle trust/stat animations
• tactile button states
• elegant loading/skeleton states

Never use distracting or childish animation. Respect reduced-motion settings.

SIMPLICITY:
Every element must earn its place.
Prefer one excellent interaction over three mediocre ones.
Use progressive disclosure.
Avoid clutter, excessive cards, unnecessary forms, excessive gradients, excessive shadows, tiny controls and pointless decoration.

Ask constantly:
“Can this interaction be reduced by one step?”

MICROCOPY:
Short, confident, human and calm:
“Clear road ahead.”
“Traffic checking reported nearby.”
“Still there?”
“Thanks — you helped nearby drivers.”
“3 live alerts on your route.”
“Mostly clear.”

DESIGN SYSTEM:
Create reusable components, variables/tokens, typography, spacing, colours, buttons, chips, cards, sheets, map markers, navigation, alerts, inputs and states. Maintain consistency across every screen.

ACCESSIBILITY:
Large touch targets, strong contrast, readable type, clear states, screen-reader-friendly hierarchy and reduced motion.

FINAL STANDARD:
Be opinionated and use professional product-design judgment. Do not add features just to make it look complicated.
